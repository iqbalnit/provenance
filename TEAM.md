## Standard — the default "about the team" description
*949 characters · 145 words*

Provenance is a team of working engineers building for the BFSI track.

Banks' transaction-monitoring systems flag suspicious activity with false-positive rates often above 95%, and analysts review every alert by hand. We're building an agentic AML triage system on Gemini and Google Cloud that gathers evidence across transactions, customer documents, sanctions lists and live adverse media, and is structurally incapable of making a claim it can't cite. A deterministic verifier rejects any uncited reasoning. When the evidence is stale or incomplete, the system escalates to a human analyst with the case file already built.

Our team brings more than a decade of building financial software at scale (payroll, money movement and enterprise platforms), together with hands-on depth in agentic AI, evaluation, cloud and data engineering. Our conviction: in regulated finance, knowing when to hand a decision to a human is a feature, not a failure.

## Notes on the wording

- **Doesn't name anyone's employer.** Naming a company in a public competition entry can read
  as that company's endorsement. "Financial software at scale" says what matters.
- **No numbers we can't show a judge.** The 95% false-positive figure is the industry
  benchmark we reproduce ourselves with the rule engine. "Zero missed suspicious cases" is
  written as the *bar* we hold, not a result. Update it once the eval proves it.
- **Team-level claims assume the planned role mix** of AI/architecture, cloud and data, and
  front-end. If the team's actual strengths differ, e.g. an AML specialist in the fourth seat,
  swap that clause in. It's worth naming.
